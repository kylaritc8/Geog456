Mushroom Movement

Fungi are among the most ecologically diverse organisms on Earth. Fungal communities contribute to nearly every terrestrial ecosystem as decomposers and nutrient cyclers, yet their distributions and responses to climate change are poorly understood compared to plants and animals. This gap is especially clear in the southeastern United States, where high biodiversity and a humid climate foster fungal richness, but long-term observational studies of those communities remain sparse. Fungal sensitivity to temperature and moisture makes these communities useful ecological indicators to reflect the changing global climate. In this map, we visualize fungal occurrence records over the last century for 11 North Carolina species overlaid with temperature change to detect spatial shifts over time.
